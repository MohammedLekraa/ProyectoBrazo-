08. System power supply

The electrical design uses independent power supplies for the microcontroller and actuators, preventing voltage drops and ESP32 reboots caused by servo current peaks.

During early tests, moving multiple servos simultaneously caused reboots. To solve this, all grounds (GND) remain strictly connected, as PWM signals require a common electrical reference.

Glove board

5500 mAh rechargeable Li-ion battery, acting as a 9 V source.

Arm board

Two 18650 Li-ion cells in series at 3.2 V (~6.4 V nominal).

Servomotors

Promax FAC-363B lab power supply at 6.5 V (~1.6 A peak draw).
